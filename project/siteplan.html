The Art of Figure Skating

Site Name

The Art of Figure Skating - This name represents a website dedicated to exploring figure skating beyond its competitive nature. It highlights the combination of athletic ability, creativity, music, performance and costume design that makes the sport unique.

Site Purpose

The website will serve as an informational fan resource for people interested in figure skating. It will introduce visitors to the different disciplines of the sport, explore the artistry behind performances and design of competition costumes.

Scenarios

  • What are the different disciplines of competitive figure skating?
  • What makes a figure skating performance memorable and artistic?
  • How are figure skating competition costumes designed?
  • Where can I learn more about the different elements of figure skating?
